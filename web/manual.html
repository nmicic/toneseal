Device 1 / Device 2 · first-transfer timeline

Device 1 starts pairing and sends the first message. Device 2 answers. Prepare and Confirm make audio; only Play packet sends it. Start the listener before every playback.

  1. 1 · Send the pairing offer

    Device 1: choose Encrypted message, then Start pairing. Device 2: choose Encrypted message, then Answer pairing → Listen. Device 1: Play packet. Device 2: after the sound ends, Stop & decode.

    Device 2 should now show “Pairing answer” prepared.
  2. 2 · Send the pairing answer back

    Device 1: Listen. Device 2: Play packet. Device 1: after the sound ends, Stop & decode.

    Both devices should now display the same eight fingerprint groups.
  3. 3 · Compare, then send Device 1’s confirmation

    Both: compare every fingerprint group. If all match, tap “All groups match · confirm peer” on each device. Device 2: Listen. Device 1: Play packet. Device 2: after the sound ends, Stop & decode.

    Confirmation is not sent by tapping Confirm alone. Device 2 still needs to send its own confirmation.
  4. 4 · Send Device 2’s confirmation back

    Device 1: Listen. Device 2: Play packet. Device 1: after the sound ends, Stop & decode.

    Both devices should now show “READY TO SEND”.
  5. 5 · Send your first message

    Device 1: type “Hello” in Write your message, then Prepare encrypted message. Device 2: Listen. Device 1: Play packet. Device 2: after the sound ends, Stop & decode.

    “Hello” should appear under Received on Device 2. An empty input keeps Prepare disabled even after pairing.
  6. 6 · Return the delivery receipt

    Device 1: Listen. Device 2: Play packet to send its prepared receipt. Device 1: after the sound ends, Stop & decode.

    Device 1 should show “Delivery confirmed”. For a password, switch to Password transfer and repeat steps 5–6; then tap Copy on Device 2. No new pairing is needed.
  1. 1 · Prepare on Device 1

    Both: choose Cleartext lab. Device 1: type “Hello”, then Prepare cleartext message. No pairing is needed.

  2. 2 · Listen first, then play

    Device 2: Listen and allow microphone access. Device 1: Play packet.

  3. 3 · Decode on Device 2

    Device 2: wait until the sound ends, then Stop & decode. Look for “Hello” under Received. Cleartext has no delivery receipt.

If you see “No packet recovered”, stay on the same step: the receiver taps Listen again, the sender replays the same packet, then the receiver taps Stop & decode. Keep both pages visible. For a fresh start, tap Disconnect on both devices.

Before you send a real secret
This is an experimental protocol, not an audited password manager. Test with dummy messages first. Nearby devices can hear, record or interfere with the sound. Encryption protects message content after you verify the peer; it cannot protect a compromised device or clipboard.
There is no message server, analytics, or stored message history. The page downloads its application files over HTTPS. Use a trusted local copy served on localhost for an offline computer. A hidden, rotating URL is a convenience, not access control.
Keep both pages visible and unlocked. Use short messages, a quiet room, and a comfortable volume. An encrypted receipt confirms delivery: play the prepared receipt back to the sender. If a message or receipt is lost, choose Resend unacknowledged. The receiver acknowledges repeats without delivering them twice. Microphone capture stops after 20 seconds.

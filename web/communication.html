Advanced/Cleartext experiment

Text through sound

Experimental speaker-to-microphone messaging with JotTune Synth. This decodes a fixed tone alphabet, not music or speech.

Cleartext, unauthenticated. Anyone nearby can record or send these messages. CRC checks accidental corruption; it does not prove who sent a message. Do not send secrets. Captures stay on this device unless you choose to save them.

Send

App playback: 100%. Use your device’s volume buttons to adjust the speaker.

Start at a comfortable level. The receiving device should start capture before you press Play.

Receive

Start capture, play a message on the other device, then stop and decode. Capture stops after 20 seconds. Microphone permission is requested only by Start capture.

Nothing is recording. Prepare a message or choose a received WAV.

Decoded text

The receiver uses a fixed 48 kHz clock; nominal 44.1 kHz input is resampled. It does not estimate device clock drift. Noise, echoes and audio processing may prevent recovery. A successful local file test is not proof of an over-the-air link. No encryption, pairing, acknowledgements or automatic retries are implemented.
